How it works

Shop like you always do.

  1. 01

    Drop it in

    Put tagged groceries straight into your bag as you shop. No scanning, no queue.

  2. 02

    It knows

    Each item shows up in the app with the store’s price, so you always know your total.

  3. 03

    Walk out

    Leave the store and you’re charged automatically. Your receipt arrives on your phone, and the light on the bag confirms it. During early access, payments are simulated.

FAQ

Questions.

Will I be charged?

No. During early access, payments are simulated and no card is ever entered or charged. Receipts show a demo card.

Where can I use Bag It?

In stores that are set up for it. During early access that’s a small number of demo stores, and bags aren’t on sale yet. Anywhere else, the app won’t price or charge your bag.

What if I put in something the store doesn’t sell?

The app shows it as not sold here, and you aren’t charged for it.

Why does the app need my location?

To know which store you’re in and when you’ve left, so it can price your bag and send your receipt. There’s no account: the app uses an anonymous identity. During early access, your phone’s latest location and your trips are stored as test data that anyone can read.

The app can’t find my bag.

Make sure your bag is powered and nearby, turn on Bluetooth, allow Nearby devices for the app, then tap Connect bag.
