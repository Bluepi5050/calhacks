SmartBag device + app

Your groceries.Your bag.Just walk out.

Bag It pairs a Bluetooth smart bag with the SmartBag phone app. Everything you put in the bag lands on your list. When you leave a participating store, the bag is totalled and your receipt appears. No checkout line, no Pay button.

01 · How it works

Three steps, no checkout.

The bag knows what’s inside it, your phone knows which store you’re in, and the store’s catalog knows the prices. Leaving the store is the only “checkout” you do.

STEP 1

Connect

Switch on your SmartBag and open the app. The phone pairs with the bag over Bluetooth and, once you’re inside a participating store, shows the store’s name.

STEP 2

Fill

Shop as usual and put items straight into the bag. Each one appears on your list with the store’s price. Take something out and it comes off the list.

STEP 3

Leave

Walk out. Once your phone is clearly outside the store area, the bag is totalled, a receipt opens in the app, you get a notification and the bag’s light blinks to confirm.

02 · Get started

Set up once, in about five minutes.

Install the app, grant the permissions it needs to see your bag and your location, and keep it running while you shop.

ESP32-based bag that talks Bluetooth Low Energy and appears as SmartBag-01.

  • An Android phone with Bluetooth and GPS
  • Mobile data or Wi‑Fi while you shop
  • A SmartBag that is switched on and powered
  • A participating store

  1. Download the APK

    On your Android phone, tap Download for Android on this page and open the file when it finishes.

  2. Allow the install

    Android asks before installing apps from outside Google Play. Allow it for your browser or file manager, then tap Install.

    Usually under Settings › Apps › Special app access › Install unknown apps. Wording varies by manufacturer.

  3. Open SmartBag and tap Get started

    No account or sign-up is needed. The app creates its own anonymous identity.

  4. Allow Bluetooth

    Grant Nearby devices (Bluetooth) so the app can find and talk to your bag.

  5. Allow precise location

    Choose While using the app and keep Precise switched on. Location is how the app recognises the store and notices that you’ve left; approximate location isn’t accurate enough.

  6. Allow notifications

    SmartBag shows an ongoing “shopping” notification while it works, and a notification with your total after you walk out.

  7. Switch on the bag and keep the app running

    Keep the bag close until it shows as connected. You can lock the phone and pocket it; the ongoing notification keeps SmartBag active. Don’t force-stop the app mid-shop.

03 · Shopping & receipts

What happens in the aisle, and at the door.

Your list updates as the bag reports items. The total is only settled once you leave, using the prices of the store you were in.

In a participating store

The store’s name appears at the top of the app. Items are priced from that store’s catalog as soon as they go in the bag.

Adding and removing

Put an item in to add it; take it out to remove it. Scanning the same tagged item twice doesn’t count it twice.

Items the store doesn’t list

Anything without a price here is marked “not sold here” and is not charged. Sort it out with staff before you leave.

Outside a store

The bag still reports items, but nothing is priced until your phone is inside a participating store.

04 · For store operators

Setting up a participating store.

Bag It only works where a store has been configured. During the prototype, stores are set up together with the project team; there is no self-serve sign-up yet.

Location

Store area

A centre point and a detection radius (up to 400 m) that covers the building. Shoppers inside it are placed in your store automatically.

Catalog

Items & prices

Every product needs an item code that matches the tag the bag reads, a name and a price. Price edits apply immediately.

Staff

Access by invite

Staff join a store with a private invite code from an existing staff member. Codes can be rotated at any time.

Console

Live view

See shoppers currently in the store, a live feed of bag activity and orders from the last two days.

Before shoppers arrive

  • Place the centre at the middle of the shop floor and size the radius to cover the building, not the car park.
  • Tag each product with the code that’s in your catalog, and spot-check a few in a bag.
  • Do one full test trip: fill a bag, walk out, and confirm the order appears.

Good to know

  • Walk-out is confirmed a little beyond your radius to avoid false exits at the door.
  • Items missing from your catalog are never charged; shoppers are asked to see staff.
  • Keep invite codes private. They grant access to your store’s console.
05 · Help

Questions & troubleshooting

Most problems come down to Bluetooth, precise location, or the phone being offline.

Do I ever press Pay?

No. There is no checkout screen and no Pay button. When your phone leaves a participating store, the bag is totalled and a receipt appears in the app automatically.

Am I actually being charged?

No. Bag It is a hackathon prototype and payments are simulated. The app uses a demo payment label (“Demo Visa •••• 4242”); no card is stored and no money moves. Please don’t enter real card details anywhere.

Which stores work?

Only stores that have been set up for Bag It: a location, a detection area and a catalog of item codes. When the app recognises the store you’re in, its name appears at the top of the screen. Anywhere else, items stay in your bag list but nothing is priced or charged.

The app can’t find my bag.
  • Make sure the bag is switched on and powered (charged battery or USB power).
  • Turn on Bluetooth, and allow Nearby devices (or Bluetooth) for SmartBag in Android settings.
  • Keep the phone within a couple of metres of the bag.
  • A bag connects to one phone at a time. Close SmartBag on any other phone that used this bag.
  • Still nothing: power the bag off and on, then reopen the app.
The app says I’m not in a store.
  • Check that Location is on and that SmartBag has Precise location (not Approximate).
  • GPS indoors can drift by tens of metres. Standing near the entrance or a window usually helps.
  • The store itself must be participating; see “Which stores work?”.
  • The app needs a mobile data or Wi‑Fi connection to look the store up.
An item says “not sold here”.

That item’s code isn’t in this store’s catalog, so it is not charged. It stays visible on your list and receipt. Show it to a member of staff so they can add it to the catalog or settle it with you directly.

I walked out, but no receipt appeared.
  • Leaving is confirmed after two accurate location readings outside the store area, so it can take a short while and some distance past the door.
  • The phone needs a network connection. If it was offline, the app catches up once it reconnects; receipts are not guaranteed to be instant, and nothing is charged while the phone is offline.
  • Keep SmartBag running: don’t swipe away its ongoing notification or force-stop it. Open the app to refresh.
  • If location reporting stops with the screen off, set SmartBag’s battery usage to Unrestricted.
How do I remove an item?

Take it out of the bag. The bag reports the removal and the item leaves your list. You can also remove a line in the app.

Android won’t install the APK.
  • Allow your browser or file manager to install apps: Settings › Apps › Special app access › Install unknown apps (wording varies by phone).
  • Play Protect may warn about an unrecognised developer. That’s expected for a prototype that isn’t on Google Play; only continue if you downloaded the file from this site.
  • If an older SmartBag build is installed and the update fails, uninstall it first.
Is there an iPhone app?

Not in the App Store or TestFlight. The iPhone app currently exists as source code you can build yourself on a Mac with Xcode 15 or newer and an iPhone on iOS 16+. It has not yet been tested on a physical iPhone with the bag.

06 · Privacy & limitations

An honest prototype.

Bag It was built at Cal Hacks 13.0 in October 2026. It shows how walk-out shopping could work; it is not a commercial payment service.

Demo data only. The prototype’s database tables are currently publicly readable. Don’t enter your real name, card numbers or any other personal or payment information.

What the app sends

  • An anonymous app identity. No account, name or email.
  • Your phone’s location while a shopping session is active, to recognise the store and detect walking out.
  • The item codes your bag reports.
  • A demo payment label. No card number is ever collected.

How long it’s kept

  • The live bag activity log is cleared after about an hour.
  • Idle, empty shopping sessions are removed after about 12 hours.
  • Receipts are kept as demo orders.
  • This website sets no cookies and loads no analytics or third-party scripts.

Known limitations

  • Payments are simulated. Nothing is charged.
  • GPS, Bluetooth and mobile data can be unreliable indoors; detection and receipts are not guaranteed to be instant or to work offline.
  • The Android app hasn’t yet been verified end to end on a physical phone with the bag; the iPhone app is source-only.
  • Works only in configured stores.
Technical notes
  • Backend: a SpacetimeDB module that places a phone in a store from its location, prices the bag and records the order in a single transaction when the phone leaves.
  • The phone app makes request/response calls over one WebSocket; it does not subscribe to database updates.
  • The bag uses the Bluetooth LE Nordic UART service; prototype firmware runs on an ESP32.
